One shared record

Before you reach out, see what your team already knows.

Every company keeps its history across mandates. When a name you are about to approach is already on another live mandate, the company record says so, with the engagement, its status and the date of first contact.

Continuity

Keep the work moving, even when people move on.

Projects, contacts, follow-ups and activity stay in the firm’s workspace, not in someone’s personal files. When work changes hands, the next person opens the same record.

For the person leaving, their work stays credited to them. For the person arriving, they start with context, not a blank sheet.

Control stays with the firm.

  • Firm-scoped

    Every record belongs to your firm’s workspace and to nobody else’s.

  • Visible by mandate

    Analysts see the mandates they are assigned to. Partners see every mandate.

  • Sent from your own mailbox

    Outreach goes from a connected Gmail or Outlook account when a person chooses to send, or is logged from wherever it was sent.

  • Revocable sessions

    Sign-in is held in secure cookies, and sessions can be revoked.
