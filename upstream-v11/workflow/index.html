Getting started

Bring the work into one shared record.

Start from the spreadsheets your team already keeps. Upstream reads the whole client workbook and shows you what it found before anything is written.

  1. 1

    Upload the workbooks

    Your company lists, outreach tracker and contact sheets, together.

  2. 2

    Map the project

    Choose the project and engagements each sheet belongs to.

  3. 3

    Review before applying

    See the companies, contacts and outreach history it found, and resolve anything it cannot safely match.

  4. 4

    Keep working

    Log the outreach you already do. Follow-ups, history and reporting build from it.
