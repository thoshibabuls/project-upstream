FAQ

Questions teams ask.

What does Upstream do today?

It runs the work around a mandate: projects and engagements, the firm’s company database, contacts, sourcing, an outreach desk with follow-up dates worked out for you, analytics and Excel import.

Does Upstream read our emails?

No. Today it can send an email from a connected Gmail or Outlook account when a person chooses to, and it records the outreach your team logs. Reading email and calendar is a future phase, and will need each person’s permission.

Can we start with one mandate?

Yes, and we recommend it. Bring the spreadsheets for one live mandate, and the rest can follow when you are ready.

Can we bring our existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Upload the client workbooks, map each sheet to a project and its engagements, and review what Upstream found before anything is written.

Who can see what?

Analysts see the mandates they are assigned to. Partners see everything. Access is checked on every list, so a mandate you are not on does not load.

More questions about setup, sending and what comes next
What happens when someone leaves?

Their projects, contacts, follow-ups and activity stay in the firm’s workspace, credited to them, and the next person is given access to the mandate.

Does Upstream send sequences or mass email?

No. Each email is written or reviewed and sent by a person, and the follow-up schedule tells them when.

What is coming next?

Relationship intelligence: memory drawn from email and calendar with each person’s permission, warm introduction paths and better recommendations. The roadmap above lists what is available now and what comes later.

Where is our data stored?

We are confirming data residency and our position under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act with counsel, and will settle both with you before any firm pays.
